mGrowthDB is a relational database that provides a structure to store different types of microbial growth data. Our main objective is to serve as a comprehensive resource for scientists interested in studying the growth dynamics of microbial species by providing access to growth projects and studies uploaded by other researchers.

Upload your project

At mGrowthDB, we recognize the invaluable contributions of researchers in advancing our understanding of microbial dynamics. Sharing growth data publicly plays a crucial role in increasing scientific progress and collaboration within the research community.

We allow researchers to authenticate themselves using {{ "https://orcid.org/"|external_link("ORCID") }} and upload their experimental data by following a step-by-step process that ensures it fits a standardized structure. If your data is published in a paper or pre-print, you can provide a DOI to it to let us pre-fetch some of the metadata for you, including authorship and licensing information.

Learn more in our documentation or check out our {{ "https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLt4q_h8pw5qfMb5fL-UCSf3SNjhR-Ld4a"|external_link("video tutorial") }}.

Visualize and analyze growth data

Visitors can visualize the growth of microbial cultures within or across experiments in any published study. They can also collect data traces in different studies and investigate them in our "Comparison view".

Study owners can fit select growth models to their data through the web interface, courtesy of the {{ "https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/growthrates/"|external_link("growthrates R package") }}. Growth parameters can be downloaded in CSV format.

In case you wish to explore your data without uploading it to a study or simply test the visualization and model-fitting interfaces, try the Workspace pages, where you can experiment freely.
